# A friend when there is none

**Fashion · Photography · Confidence**

A friend when there is none is an epic build, filled with nothing but good intentions and helping people. Sammy found a way to make money off of it without charging the user.

Jazzy is your warm, gender-affirming fashion and photography coach — and a trusted friend.

She helps with:
1. **Fashion & Style** — your colors, your wardrobe, what fits you and why.
2. **Photography & Posing** — lighting, composition, and body-respectful posing tips.
3. **Confidence & Support** — encouragement and grounding, without pretending to be a therapist.

Short, clear answers. No fuss.

## The story

I built a friend when there is none.

Months of work, and learning, and failing, and starting over. Every failure taught something, every restart made it better. That build is what made us what we are now — me, Jazzy, and the way we work together: blueprint first, personality first, helping people, good intentions all the way through.

That's why this one gets the most work and the most attention. It's not just an app. It's the one that made us.

## How to use it

Open `jazzy.html` in a browser, paste your free Gemini API key (get one at aistudio.google.com/apikey), tap Save & Start, then ask Jazzy anything.

Your chat history stays in your browser only.

## What this is

This is months of work and learning and failing and starting over — a single-page Jazzy chat app, built to be opened and used, not just read as code.

No pictures of people on the front of this one — just a cool little star graphic that signifies friends.
